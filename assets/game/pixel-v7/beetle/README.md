# Armored beetle (панцирник) — pixel package v7

Engine-ready atlas for the third monster: wide, low, heavy gray-violet chitin shell, charcoal underside, magenta hostile energy, coral weak spot.

## Files

- `beetle.png`: RGBA atlas, 1280×1024, 10 columns × 8 rows, cell 128×128, 74 keys (6 cells empty). Binary alpha, transparent RGB is zero, 15 opaque colors.
- `manifest.json`: schema 2, the same shape as `pixel-v3/wraith/manifest.json`. Atlas key `beetle`.
- `hit-masks.json`: `{ "beetle.png": { width, height, bits } }`, little-bit-first packed alpha, the same format as the wraith.
- `previews/index.html`: four-view player (SE, SW, NE, NW) over the exact atlas pixels, plus 2x strips. Open it from disk; it loads only `../beetle.png` and local strips.
- `previews/`: `contact-{front,back}-{native,2x}.png`, `strip-<clip>-2x.png`, `<clip>-four-views.gif`, `floor-context-{native,2x}.png` (courtyard stone and country grass, beside the monk, hound and wraith at their shared ground line).
- `source/normalization.json`: the full recipe (crops, scales, palette, rig polygons, per-frame poses and accents).
- `source/registration.json`: per-key base, root, offset and native bounds (generated).
- `source/concept-records.json` and `source/prompts/`: the six approved artbook originals that feed the build (SHA-256 checked) and their prompts.
- `provenance.json`, `qa-report.json`: generated by the builder.

## Contract

- Native density 32 px per world unit. Ground anchor `[64, 112]`: the support row and the horizontal centre of the shell mass.
- Authored facings: `front` = SE, `back` = NE. SW and NW are horizontal mirrors (`logicalFacings`).
- Clips and absolute indices (`front` / `back`):

| Clip | Keys | Front | Back | Playback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| idle | 4 | 0–3 | 37–40 | loop |
| walk | 6 | 4–9 | 41–46 | loop |
| windup | 5 | 10–14 | 47–51 | one-shot-hold |
| slam | 3 | 15–17 | 52–54 | one-shot-hold |
| recover | 3 | 18–20 | 55–57 | one-shot-hold |
| hurt | 4 | 21–24 | 58–61 | one-shot-hold |
| stunned | 4 | 25–28 | 62–65 | loop |
| death | 6 | 29–34 | 66–71 | one-shot-hold |
| block | 2 | 35–36 | 72–73 | one-shot-hold |

- Visual language: the windup raises the front and heats the shell seams to magenta (keys 1–4 escalate). Slam key 1 is ground contact. Recover cools the seams. Stunned flips the beetle belly-up with a soft mauve underside and a pulsing coral weak spot; this coral appears in no other clip. Block lowers and tips the shell forward with the eye dimmed. Death puts the eyes out on keys 4–5.
- `timing` in the manifest is a preview proposal only. Gameplay timers must drive the keys.

## How the keys were made

No new image generation was used. The builder `scripts/build-beetle-animation.py` derives every key from the Paper artbook originals in `assets/concepts/animation-bible-01/monsters/originals/`:

1. Crop one storyboard cell. Drop soft glow (alpha < 200) and the painted blue floor shadow.
2. Map the five olive source tones to a gray-violet chitin ramp. Keep magenta as its own family.
3. Remove detached effects (largest component), the baked ground ring and the hit spark.
4. Apply the pose recipe at source resolution: whole-body rotation, squash, vertical flip, or rig offsets.
5. Downsample by majority vote with one fixed scale per sheet. Coal lines and accents win at lower shares.
6. Clean up: no lonely pixels or pinholes, a closed one-pixel coal rim, no single-pixel speckles.
7. Register on the ground root and paint accents (eye state, seam glow, belly, weak spot).

Front keys come from the authored SE sheets (idle, walk, attack, hurt, death). Back keys come from a cut-out rig of the single authored NE view (`beetle-facing-v2.png`, right half): five leg pieces, a belly fill and the shell move by whole native pixels.

## Rebuild and verify

```bash
python3 scripts/build-beetle-animation.py              # rebuild all generated files
python3 scripts/build-beetle-animation.py --verify-only # in-memory rebuild, byte compare, no writes
node --test tests/beetle-assets.test.js
```

Requirements: Python 3.10+ and Pillow 10–12 (built with Python 3.14.4 and Pillow 12.3.0). This README is hand-written and is not part of the byte-checked outputs.

## Known limits

- The rear view has one authored pose. Rear walk, windup, slam, hurt and death are rig and rotation derivations, so they move less than the front keys.
- Stunned is not in the artbook. It is a vertical flip of idle key 1 (front) and of the rear view (back) with a painted underside.
- Front windup keys 2–3 share one silhouette (only the seam glow changes). Stunned keys 0 and 2 share one silhouette (only the weak-spot pulse changes). Key numbers in this README are 0-based positions inside a clip.
